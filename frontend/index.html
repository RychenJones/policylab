Model notes

Current assumptions

  • The population contains 5,000 independently modeled households.
  • Household characteristics are synthetic and randomized.
  • Each household receives one primary transportation outcome.
  • Cost and emissions results are annual estimates.
  • Policy results are exploratory and should not be treated as forecasts.
